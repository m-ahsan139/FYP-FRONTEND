Article 6 — High Treason

Constitution of Pakistan, 1973

Article 6 of the Constitution of Pakistan is one of the most serious provisions in the supreme law of the land.

6(1): Any person who abrogates or subverts or suspends or holds in abeyance, or attempts or conspires to abrogate or subvert or suspend or hold in abeyance, the Constitution by use of force or show of force or by other unconstitutional means shall be guilty of high treason.

6(2): Any person aiding or abetting or collaborating in the acts mentioned in clause (1) shall likewise be guilty of high treason.

6(3): Majlis-e-Shoora (Parliament) shall by law provide for the punishment of persons found guilty of high treason. Punishment shall not be less than life imprisonment.

This article was strengthened by the 18th Constitutional Amendment (2010) to ensure that future unconstitutional takeovers cannot be validated by any court.
